ABOUT BLUEPRINT

System design, made approachable.

Blueprint is a free, structured learning path for understanding how reliable, scalable software is put together. It turns a broad subject into approachable steps and points you to trusted resources along the way.

01

Start with fundamentals

Learn networking, APIs, and data before jumping into distributed systems. A clear foundation makes every later trade-off easier to understand.

02

Follow the connections

System design is about how components work together. Move through the roadmap in order, and revisit earlier topics whenever you need to.

03

Practice by explaining

Use the case studies to sketch a design, talk through trade-offs, and identify what you would improve as the system grows.

READY WHEN YOU ARE

There is no finish line for learning.

Pick a topic, use its resource, and track your progress as you build understanding.
